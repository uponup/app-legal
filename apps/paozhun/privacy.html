隐私政策 — 跑准

最后更新日期：2026 年 11 月 22 日
发布者：Three Thousand Fighting Soldiers

跑准是一款在 iPhone 上使用的跑步配速工具。本政策说明我们如何处理与本应用相关的信息，以及哪些信息不会被收集。App Store 隐私营养标签目标：Data Not Collected（我们不会从此 App 中收集数据）。

1. 摘要

  • 跑准没有自有服务器，也不要求注册或登录。
  • 本应用免费：无订阅、无付费解锁、无广告、无应用内购买。
  • 跑步记录、配速、分段和设置只保存在你的设备上。
  • 不接入第三方广告、分析或追踪 SDK。
  • 我们不会把个人数据收集到自有服务器，因为没有自有服务器。

2. 本机数据

你在应用中设定的目标配速、每次跑步的距离、时间、配速、每公里分段、历史记录以及相关偏好，都保存在你的设备本地。我们无法从远程访问这些数据。卸载本应用后，设备上的本应用数据将按系统规则被清除（你自行另外保存的副本除外）。

3. 位置信息

只有在你主动开始一次跑步后，本应用才会使用设备的定位服务，用于计算配速、距离和每公里分段。这包括屏幕锁定或应用处于后台时继续获取位置，以便锁屏或息屏后仍能记录本次跑步。我们不会在你没有跑步时在后台持续定位。

位置数据只用于完成本次跑步记录，并保存在你的设备本地。我们不会把位置信息上传到自有服务器。

在 Apple 的 App 隐私营养标签中，「收集」是指将数据传出设备，并由我们或第三方留存。跑准的位置与跑步记录只在本机处理，不会传出设备由我们留存，因此营养标签为 Data Not Collected（我们不会从此 App 中收集数据）。即便如此，本政策仍会清楚说明本机如何使用位置信息。

你可以在 iPhone 的「设置 > 隐私与安全性 > 定位服务」中变更或关闭权限。若关闭定位，将无法正常记录跑步和配速。

4. 运动与体能（如系统请求）

若本应用申请「运动与体能」权限，仅用于辅助判断运动状态、提高记录稳定性。相关信息同样只保留在本机，不会上传。

5. 无账号、云同步或自有服务器

跑准不创建账号、不要求登录，也不把数据同步到开发者运营的云端。本应用没有用于运营本服务的云端账号系统，也没有自有服务器。数据只留在这台 iPhone 上。

6. 导出与分享（如你自行操作）

若你在本机导出或分享某次跑步记录，内容只会到达你选择的目的地（例如系统分享面板或你保存的文件）。跑准不会将其上传到自有服务器。

7. 外部链接

本应用可能会在 Safari 中打开本隐私政策或其他帮助链接。这些页面是静态的法律或帮助文档。打开它们会使用设备的常规网络连接，不涉及应用账号。

8. 系统诊断（Apple）

如果你允许 Apple 收集系统诊断，崩溃报告可能会按你的 iOS 隐私设置发送给 Apple。跑准不内置第三方分析或崩溃统计 SDK。

9. 儿童

跑准不以 13 岁以下儿童为对象，也不会故意收集儿童的个人信息。

10. 政策更新

如本政策有更新，上方日期将被修订。发布到本公开法律站点后，最新版本将始终可在本网址查阅。

11. 联系我们

如对本政策或个人信息处理有疑问，请通过 App Store Connect（跑准应用支持）或 App Store 产品页上列出的联系方式联系开发者。

Privacy Policy — 跑准

Last updated: 22 November 2026
Publisher: Three Thousand Fighting Soldiers

跑准 is a running-pace tool for iPhone. This policy explains what data is handled and what is not. Target App Store privacy nutrition label: Data Not Collected.

1. Summary

  • 跑准 has no first-party server and does not require an account or login.
  • The app is free: no subscriptions, no paid unlocks, no ads, and no in-app purchases.
  • Run records, pace, splits, and settings stay on your device.
  • No third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not collect personal data from you for our own servers, because there are none.

2. On-device data

The target pace you set, each run’s distance, time, pace, kilometer splits, history, and related preferences are stored locally on your device. We cannot remotely access that data. Uninstalling the app removes it from the device (subject to any copies you made yourself).

3. Location

Location services are used only after you start a run, to compute pace, distance, and splits. This includes continuing to use location while the screen is locked or the app is in the background, so the run can still be recorded when the display is off. We do not keep locating you in the background when you are not running.

Location data is used to complete that run record and is stored on your device. We do not upload location data to our servers.

In Apple’s App Privacy nutrition label, “collect” means transmitting data off the device so that we or a third party can retain it. 跑准 processes location and run records on-device only and does not send them off-device for us to retain, so the nutrition label is Data Not Collected. This policy still explains how location is used on the device.

You can change or turn off permission in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services. If location access is off, the app cannot record runs and pace normally.

4. Motion & Fitness (if the system asks)

If the app requests Motion & Fitness access, it is only to help detect activity state and improve recording stability. That information also stays on the device and is not uploaded.

5. No account, cloud sync, or first-party server

跑准 does not create accounts, sign you in, or sync data to a developer-operated cloud. There is no first-party server and no cloud account system for this service. Your data stays on this iPhone.

6. Export and sharing (optional, user-initiated)

If you export or share a run from the device, that content goes only to the destination you choose (for example the system Share Sheet or a file you save). 跑准 does not upload it to our servers.

7. External links

The app may open this privacy policy or other help links in Safari. Those pages are static legal or help documents. Opening them uses your device’s normal network connection; no app account is involved.

8. Crash diagnostics (Apple)

If you allow Apple system diagnostics, crash reports may go to Apple under your iOS privacy settings. 跑准 does not embed third-party analytics or crash SDKs.

9. Children

跑准 is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children’s data.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated. The latest version will remain at this URL once published on the public legal site.

11. Contact

Privacy questions: contact the developer via App Store Connect (跑准 app support) or the email listed on the App Store product page.

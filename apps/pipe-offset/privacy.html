Privacy Policy — Pipe Offset

Last updated: 8 November 2026
Publisher: Three Thousand Fighting Soldiers

Pipe Offset is a free, offline industrial pipefitter offset calculator for iOS. This policy explains what data is handled and what is not. Target App Store privacy nutrition label: Data Not Collected.

1. Summary

  • Pipe Offset has no first-party server and does not require an account or login.
  • The app is free: no subscriptions, no paid unlocks, no ads, and no in-app purchases.
  • Calculation inputs, results history, and settings stay on your device.
  • No third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not collect personal data from you for our own servers, because there are none.

2. On-device data

Information you enter for calculations (for example offset distances, elbow angle, optional takeout / weld gap values), recent calculation history, and preferences such as units or appearance are stored locally on your device. We cannot remotely access that data. Uninstalling the app removes it from the device (subject to any copies you made yourself).

3. No account, cloud sync, or network features

Pipe Offset does not create accounts, sign you in, or sync to a developer-operated cloud. Version 1 does not use iCloud / CloudKit for app data. The calculator is designed to work offline, including Airplane Mode.

4. Sharing (optional)

If you choose Share, the iOS system Share Sheet may send a plain-text summary you selected to another app you pick. Pipe Offset does not upload that content to our servers.

5. External links

The app may open this privacy policy or other help links in Safari. Those pages are static legal / help documents. Opening them uses your device’s normal network connection; no app account is involved.

6. Crash diagnostics (Apple)

If you allow Apple system diagnostics, crash reports may go to Apple under your iOS privacy settings. Pipe Offset does not embed third-party analytics or crash SDKs.

7. Children

Pipe Offset is a trade calculation tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children’s data.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated. The latest version will remain at this URL once published on the public legal site.

9. Contact

Privacy questions: contact the developer via App Store Connect (Pipe Offset app support) or the email listed on the App Store product page.
